# Commercial licensing

`mobile-qa` is released under the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0** (see `LICENSE.md`).

## What that means

**Free, no permission needed:**

- personal use, study, and experimentation
- research and teaching
- use by a charitable organization, and
- use by a government body

**Requires a paid license from the author:**

- use by a company or by a sole proprietor, including internal use
- use in the development, testing or release of a commercial product
- running it in CI for a product that is sold or monetized in any way
- redistributing it, or any work based on it, as part of a commercial offering

The license defines this precisely under *Noncommercial Purposes* — the short version is that
if your company benefits from it, it needs a license.

## Getting a license

Write to **mirmohsun.mamedov@gmail.com** with:

- the company name and what the product is,
- roughly how many engineers and how many applications would use the skill,
- whether you need it in CI, on developer machines, or both.

Pricing is per company and per year, and depends on those answers. A license can include
priority fixes and support for your own project's flows.

## Why not MIT

The skill is maintained, and the intention is to keep developing it: more rules, Android support,
and autotests that can file their own tickets. A permissive license would make it free for
companies to build on it with nothing coming back to maintain it. Noncommercial keeps it open to
read, learn from and use privately, while commercial users share the cost of keeping it alive.

If the noncommercial terms block something you believe should be allowed, write — the answer may
simply be yes.
